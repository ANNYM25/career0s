# The 7-month strategy — Nov 2026 → June 2027

Goal: a **signed offer** in Sustainable Finance / FinTech (early-career, Europe)
by June 2027. We treat the job hunt like a business-development pipeline, not a
lottery of mass applications.

## The one principle

> Don't measure success by *applications sent*. Measure it by **relevant
> conversations, quality applications, and interviews generated per week.**

Automation does the *searching and form-filling*. You spend your time on the two
things that actually convert: **networking** and **interview preparation**.

## Weekly targets (not rigid quotas)

| Activity | Weekly target | Who does it |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Quality, well-matched applications | **10–15** | Career0S preps · you submit |
| Personalised networking requests | 15–25 | **You** (manual, LinkedIn) |
| Meaningful professional conversations | 2–5 | You (coffee chats) |
| LinkedIn posts | 1–2 | You draft w/ AI, you publish |
| Follow-ups | 5–10 | You |

10–15 *tailored* applications beat 50 generic ones for specialist roles.

## Role families (priority order)

1. **Sustainable Finance / ESG Advisory**
2. Financial Analyst / FP&A
3. FinTech / Financial Services
4. ESG Reporting / Sustainability Assurance
5. Risk, Data & Financial Analysis
6. Strategy & Management Consulting

These are encoded in `config/anny.yaml` and drive the scoring engine.

## Phase plan

| Month | Focus | Career0S milestone |
|-------|-------|--------------------|
| **Nov 2026** | Foundation | Import universe, resolve feeds, daily discovery live. Polish 2 CVs. Build LinkedIn presence. |
| **Dec 2026** | Pipeline fills | First 10–15 apps/week. Start networking (10 requests/day manually). Winter internship / spring deadlines. |
| **Jan–Feb 2027** | Peak application season | Most graduate/intern deadlines land here. Lean hard on the daily report. Convert networking → coffee chats. |
| **Mar 2027** | Interviews | Shift time from applying to interview prep. Keep discovery running for late postings. |
| **Apr 2027** | Offers & negotiation | Final rounds. Compare offers. |
| **May–Jun 2027** | Close | Accept the best offer. |

## Division of labour

**Automated (Career0S, free):** discovery across 540 companies' public feeds,
scoring/shortlisting, cover-letter drafts, application form-prep, daily reports,
Obsidian tracking.

**Human (you, always):** final submit, all LinkedIn networking, coffee chats,
interview prep, answering eligibility/work-rights/declaration questions.

## What we deliberately avoid

- No LinkedIn scraping or auto-messaging (account risk + prohibited).
- No auto-submitting applications (legal declarations need a human).
- No mass generic applications (hurts specialist candidacy).
- No paid tools of any kind.

## Daily routine (15 min + application time)

1. `career0s run` (or let `launchd` do it overnight).
2. Open the **Daily Opportunity Report** in Obsidian.
3. Pick the roles worth your time; `career0s prepare <id>` for each.
4. Review every field, submit yourself.
5. Send your networking requests manually.
